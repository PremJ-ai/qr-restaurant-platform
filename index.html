Customer flow

Designed for QR-first mobile use
1. ScanTable QR opens the route.
2. BrowseCategory navigation + search.
3. CustomizeSize, shots, crust, toppings.
4. OrderServer validates + prices.
5. TrackLive status to completion.

Edge-case handling

QR table context, stale menu protection, unavailable items, incompatible options, quantity limits, invalid customer data, duplicate checkout protection and server-side total recalculation are part of the intended production flow.

Service separation

Next.js owns customer UX, Angular owns the staff portal, Node owns HTTP/request orchestration, and Python owns business workflow/state transitions.
